The GPU pod is stopped

PitchVision runs on a RunPod GPU that stops itself when idle so it doesn't bill while nobody is using it. Nothing is lost — uploads, results and models live on a persistent volume.

To wake it: open the RunPod console → Pods → Start. It's back in about 30 seconds; this page refreshes itself every 30 s.

Still here after a minute? The pod may be on its first boot (installing dependencies takes ~10 min) — check the pod's Logs for [pitchvision] starting server.
